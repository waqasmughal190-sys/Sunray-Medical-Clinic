What We Treat

Everyday care, handled properly.

From a child's fever to a work medical check, Sunray covers the health needs that come up most — with the same doctor getting to know your family over time.

General Consultation

Same-day consultations for colds, fevers, infections, aches and everyday illness.

Health Screening

Blood pressure, glucose, cholesterol and full body check-up packages.

Vaccinations

Childhood immunisation, flu shots and travel vaccinations.

Chronic Disease Care

Ongoing management for diabetes, hypertension and cholesterol.

Minor Procedures

Wound dressing, suturing, minor injuries and same-day treatment.

Medical Certificates

MCs, fit-to-work letters and pre-employment medical exams.
